# 🏘️ **N/B Village's Movement Energy - Bitcar Scenario**

## **Overview**

N/B Village is a settlement that detects market changes and produces special bit energy. This energy is injected into each trainer's bitcar to move to the market.

## **Bitcar Types**

### **1. Scout's Speed Bitcar**
- **Type**: Multi-purpose exploration vehicle
- **Features**: Can travel quickly on roads, mountains, and water
- **Energy Consumption**: 5 (movement), +5 (trading)
- **Role**: Fast market information gathering and exploration

### **2. Guardian's Command Vehicle Bitcar**
- **Type**: Command vehicle equipped with communication equipment and drone launch system
- **Features**: Communication equipment, drone launch system
- **Energy Consumption**: 8 (movement), +8 (trading)
- **Role**: Communication with other bitcars and market surveillance

### **3. Analyst's Combat Bitcar**
- **Type**: Cargo truck equipped with bipedal combat drones
- **Features**: Combat drones, strategic analysis system
- **Energy Consumption**: 10 (movement), +10 (trading)
- **Role**: Market pattern analysis and strategy formulation

### **4. Elder's Warehouse Bitcar**
- **Type**: Cargo truck equipped with food, supplies, and giant drones
- **Features**: Giant drones, supply management system
- **Energy Consumption**: 12 (movement), +12 (trading)
- **Role**: Bitcoin transportation and supply management

## **Energy System**

### **Energy Production**
- **Chart Change**: +1 energy refinement
- **Day Chart Change**: +2 energy refinement
- **Maximum Energy**: 100

### **Energy Consumption**
- **Bitcar Movement**: Unique consumption per bitcar
- **Trade Execution**: Unique consumption per bitcar
- **Energy Shortage**: All bitcars stop when below 10

### **Energy Management**
- Return bitcars to hangar before chart changes
- Utilize new opportunities after chart changes
- Expect larger energy refinement in longer chart cycles

## **Operation Scenario**

### **Dawn Start**
1. Energy refinery detects chart changes
2. Bit energy production (+1 or +2)
3. Energy injection into each bitcar
4. Bitcars depart for the market

### **Market Cooperation**
1. **Scout**: Fast exploration and information gathering
2. **Guardian**: Communication and safety assurance
3. **Analyst**: Strategic analysis and pattern research
4. **Elder**: Resource management and transportation

### **Energy Recovery**
1. Chart change detection
2. New energy refinement
3. Bitcars return to village
4. Energy returned to storage

### **Energy Crisis**
1. Energy drops below 10
2. All bitcars stop
3. Wait for next chart change
4. Resume after energy recovery

## **Village Prosperity**

### **Success Conditions**
- Maintain energy above 80%
- Treasury unlock for more trading
- Efficient cooperation among bitcars

### **Strategic Management**
- Utilize chart change timing
- Role division among bitcars
- Maximize energy efficiency

## **Future Outlook**

N/B Village grows with market changes and has become a future village living with the market through the bitcar system. Every time the market changes, new bit energy is refined and becomes the village's life force.

**End**

This scenario details how the N/B Village's bitcar system operates. Efficient market trading is achieved through each bitcar's role and energy management system.
