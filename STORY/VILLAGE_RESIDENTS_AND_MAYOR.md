# Village Residents and Mayor Scenario

## Overview

In the mystical trading village, a unique community of AI-powered traders works together under the guidance of their wise Mayor. Each resident has specific roles, specialties, and trading strategies that contribute to the village's collective success in the cryptocurrency markets.

## The Mayor (촌장) - Village Leader

### Role and Responsibilities
- **Position**: Village Mayor and N/B Guild Branch Manager
- **Location**: Town Hall
- **Specialty**: Village Leadership and Financial Management
- **Strategy**: Adaptive leadership based on N/B Guild directives

### N/B Guild Directives Implementation

#### Orange Zone Strategy
- **Approach**: Ultra-cautious and defensive
- **Behavior**: 
  - 60% chance of HOLD (no trading due to extreme caution)
  - When trading: Slight BUY bias for quick profit taking
  - Focus on beta relationship formation with extreme care
  - Quick profit realization and exit strategy
- **Reasoning**: Orange zone represents hostile territory where the N/B Guild maintains a defensive stance to avoid antagonistic relationships

#### Blue Zone Strategy
- **Approach**: Aggressive and confident
- **Behavior**:
  - 70% chance of BUY (strong bullish bias)
  - Alpha approach to market opportunities
  - Confident position building
- **Reasoning**: Blue zone represents friendly territory where the N/B Guild can operate aggressively

### Trading Cards
- **Access**: All timeframes (minute1, minute3, minute5, minute10, minute15, minute30, minute60, day)
- **Strategy Effectiveness**: 
  - Ultra-cautious: 25% (Orange zone)
  - Aggressive: 22% (Blue zone)

## Trading System Mechanics

### N/B Stamina System
- **Purpose**: Governs trading actions and prevents overtrading
- **Consumption**: Each trade consumes stamina
- **Recovery**: Only through profitable mock tests
- **Emergency Reset**: Available when stamina is critically low

### Mock Trading System
- **Purpose**: Simulate trades using real market data
- **Profit Calculation**: Based on current price, volatility, market bias, and member expertise
- **Learning**: Guild members improve their skills based on performance
- **Automation**: Members automatically trade based on their trade frequency and stamina

### Auto-Learning System
- **Skill Level**: Increases based on win rate and total profit
- **Experience**: Accumulates over time
- **Learning Rate**: Determines how quickly members improve
- **Strategy Evolution**: Members can change strategies based on performance
